# Goal

Secure a full-time design leadership position by the end of Q1 2027 by building a comprehensive application structured around the following integrated modules:

1. **User Profile Database**: Ingests uploaded documents and interview insights to maintain a master database of your experience for generating tailored resumes, interview anecdotes, and cover letters.
2. **Target Company Profiling**: Develops ideal company and role criteria—covering firmographics, growth stage, culture, and founder background—informed by vetted resources like Lenny’s Jobs and TrueUp.
3. **Automated Job Matching**: Scrapes ATS platforms and company career pages to curate a daily database of openings complete with fit scores and suitability summaries.
4. **Referral & Network Mapping**: Identifies LinkedIn connections positioned to provide warm referrals for target roles.
5. **Skill Gap Analysis & Customization**: Evaluates your background against job descriptions, highlights skill gaps with remediation strategies, and generates targeted application assets.
6. **Application Lifecycle Tracking**: Monitors application statuses and triggers reminders for time-sensitive follow-ups and submissions.
7. **Portfolio Case Study Alignment**: Maps and tags specific design case studies from your portfolio directly to the requirements of target job descriptions.
8. **Networking & Outreach Tracker**: Manages informational interviews, warm introductions, and follow-up cadences with referrals.
9. **Leadership Interview Preparation**: Provides modules for practicing design leadership assessments, including portfolio reviews, design critiques, and executive stakeholder management.
10. **Compensation & Market Benchmarking**: Evaluates and tracks salary and equity data to benchmark offers against current market rates for leadership roles.
11. **Post-Interview Feedback Loop**: Logs interview outcomes and interviewer feedback to automatically suggest profile or resume refinements for subsequent rounds.
