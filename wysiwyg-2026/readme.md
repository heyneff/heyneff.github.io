WYSIWYG brings together artists working with technology and media in ways that reveal how digital ephemera are embodied in material and infrastructure. Digital art likes to imagine itself untethered from the material world. Yet like a mind without a body, the digital is meaningless without the mediation of some IRL technology or infrastructure by which it is experienced. By placing digital artworks for sale on eBay, the show further exposes how a platform's infrastructural logic mediates and produces the object it purports only to sell. Some artists embody their work in a shippable object: a USB stick, a desktop PC, a microcontroller. Others hijack the listing itself, its images, HTML, and server infrastructure, as the work's true material. At both extremes, the show argues digital art is never immaterial, but entangled in a global economy of material extraction and platform capital.
